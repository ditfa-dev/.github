# Community code of conduct

We want Ditfa's projects to be useful, welcoming spaces for people of every background and experience level.

- Be respectful, constructive and specific. Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information, credentials or private work.
- Make room for different perspectives, abilities and levels of experience.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, impersonate others or post sexualized content or spam.

These expectations apply to Ditfa-maintained repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations or restrict participation to protect the community, based on the context and severity of a violation.

To report a conduct concern, use [Ditfa's contact page](https://ditfa.com/contact) and request a private channel. Share sensitive details only after that channel is established. Maintainers will handle reports with care and limit unnecessary disclosure.
